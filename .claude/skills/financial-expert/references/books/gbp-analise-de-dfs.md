### p01 (aulas 1-5 start: concepts, BP, DRE, double entry, start of DFC)
- Course aim: accounting concepts independent of local rules (BRGAAP, USGAAP, IFRS). Brazil: IFRS mandatory for listed firms from 2010 (timeline shows 2010: Brazilian companies must follow international norms); Lei 6.404/76 art. 177 requires Fundamental Accounting Principles; CPC definitions of asset/liability/equity used.
- Principles: entidade, continuidade (going concern), denominador comum monetário, confrontação (matching), realização da receita (earned + realized), custo histórico, essência sobre forma, objetividade, consistência, materialidade, conservadorismo (recognize expenses/losses early, revenue only when reasonably certain). Regime de competência (accrual) preferred over caixa. App: reported equity is at historical cost; book value of old assets (land, PP&E) may understate market; IFRS fair value adjustments sit in "ajustes de avaliação patrimonial".
- Accounting scandals as red flags: Enron (SPEs, off-balance debt, consolidation vs equity method choice hides liabilities); Panamericano (BR bank 2008-10 counted sold credit portfolios as its own; Caixa bought 49% voting; BTG bought stake). Lesson: audit quality (Big Four) and related party/off-balance structures; for banks check credit portfolio provisioning.
- Asset definition (CPC): controlled resource from past events with expected future economic benefits, measurable cost. Liability: present obligation, probable outflow. Equity = residual. Equation: A = L + PL. BR order of BP: by liquidity (disponível first). Circulante = up to 12 months OR operating cycle, whichever longer. Non-current: Realizável a LP, Investimentos, Imobilizado, Intangível; liabilities: Exigível a LP; PL: capital social, reservas de capital, ajustes de avaliação patrimonial, reservas de lucros (legal, estatutária, contingência, orçamentária, lucros a realizar), lucros/prejuízos acumulados, ações em tesouraria.
- Intangibles/franchise fees/goodwill (ágio) sit in assets; acquisition example: price R$10bn vs book equity R$8bn => ágio R$2bn recorded. App: P/B and tangible book: subtract goodwill/intangibles when judging asset value (esp. after M&A, e.g. education, retail rollups).
- Business cycle accounts: matéria-prima -> produção -> estoque -> venda (caixa/duplicatas); fornecedores, salários, impostos a pagar as operating liabilities.
- DRE structure BR: Receita bruta - deduções (devoluções, abatimentos, impostos sobre vendas: ICMS, IPI, PIS, COFINS, ISS) = Receita líquida - CMV = Lucro bruto - despesas operacionais (SG&A, depreciação) = Lucro operacional - despesas não operacionais/financeiras = LAIR - IR/CSLL - participações estatutárias (debêntures, administradores, empregados, partes beneficiárias) = Lucro líquido. Margins use Receita LÍQUIDA as base. Brazilian deductions can be 15-20%+ of gross revenue (ICMS 357k + IPI 149k + PIS 21k of 2,970k gross in sample). App: use net revenue for margins/PSR.
- Link DRE-BP: Lucros retidos T+1 = retidos T + lucro líquido - dividendos. Dividends in Brazil: lucro líquido minus reservas; mandatory minimum dividend per Lei 6.404 (not explicit in this deck).
- Basic ratios with benchmark vs comparables: liquidez corrente = AC/PC (J&W 1.40 vs sector 1.15); dívida/PL = (CP+LP debt)/PL (0.85 vs 1.10); margem bruta = LB/RL (53.1% vs 60.5%); margem líquida = LL/RL (9.4% vs 18.7%). Rule: always compare with sector peers, not absolute thresholds. App: sector-relative percentiles.
- Double-entry rules: debit increases assets/expenses, credit increases liabilities/PL/revenue. Depreciation reduces taxable profit but is non-cash. Net margin higher is not always better firm (ROE depends on turnover and leverage). Cost of equity > cost of debt.
- Cancelamentos/devoluções reduce Receita líquida, not gross-margin ratios below.
- DFC (cash flow statement) mandatory in BR for listed firms, large firms and S.A. closed with PL > R$2M; three flows: operating, investing, financing; direct or indirect method (indirect common in BR).
- App implications: parse DRE with net revenue base; separate operating vs financial result (financial result in BR heavily affected by Selic: big cash positions earn Selic, indebted firms pay CDI+spread); watch "participações" and non-recurring items; use DFC to verify earnings quality.
### p02 (DFC methods, vertical/horizontal analysis, ratios)
- DFC three sections: operating, investing, financing; net change + opening cash = closing cash. Direct method: receipts from sales = net sales - dAR; payments to suppliers = CMV + dEstoque - dFornecedores/Contas a pagar; minus opex and financial expenses paid. Indirect method (the one used in BR published statements): NI + D&A (+ non-cash items) - increase in operating current assets (AR, inventory, prepaid) + increase in operating current liabilities (suppliers, taxes payable, salaries). Worked case: NI 1,950 + D&A 120 + taxes payable 1,050 - inventory 500 - AR 500 + suppliers 1,000 = operating 3,120. Note: tax provision not yet paid inflates CFO. App: cash-flow statement identity; CFO = NI + non-cash - dNWC.
- Dividends classified in financing; interest paid is shown under operating in this deck (BR practice allows operating or financing; check classification before comparing CFO across firms). Purchases of shares of other companies = investing.
- Análise vertical: BP items / total assets (or total liabilities+PL); DRE items / receita líquida. Example (large BR commodity/steel-like firm): cash 27-30% of assets; PP&E 36-38%; 2011 net margin jump 16% -> 26% came from "outras receitas" 14% of revenue (non-recurring: asset disposals, provision reversals) while gross margin only 42.5% - operating profit 49% inflated. Red flag: net income growth driven by non-recurring other income and reversed provisions; also financial result -15% of revenue shows heavy leverage (R$ 20bn LT debt vs PL R$7bn). App: normalize EBIT by stripping "outras receitas/despesas operacionais" when large; compute core margin.
- Análise horizontal: base-year index (=100%); watch line items like "Outros" growing 500% (hidden liabilities), reserve de lucros shrinking while dividends/contingency provisions fall (provisions for contingencies and depósitos judiciais dropped to 37% - BR-specific litigation provisions, tax contingencies are significant in Brazil).
- Efficiency ratios (BR formulas; averages SM = saldo médio, 360 days): Giro estoque = CMV/SM estoque; PME = SM estoque/CMV x 360; Giro duplicatas = Rec bruta - (devol+abat)/SM duplicatas; PMR = SM duplicatas/(Rec bruta líquida de devol) x 360; Giro fornecedores = Compras brutas/SM fornecedores; PMP = SM fornecedores/Compras x 360; Giro AC, Giro imobilizado, Giro ativo operacional = Rec líquida / SM ativo. Compras brutas = CMV + dEstoque (+ impostos recuperáveis).
- Peers 2013 example (retailers, source Economática): Renner vs Restoque - PME 98 vs 253 days, PMR 130 vs 9 days (Renner has captive credit card receivables: financial arm inflates PMR and receivables), PMP 91 vs 120, giro ativo operacional 1.0 vs 0.7. Lesson for B3 retail: receivable days include credit-card finance receivables; compare accordingly.
- Capital structure ratios: Capital de terceiros/Capital próprio x100 (202 vs 534); Endividamento = Passivo exigível/Ativo total (67% vs 84%); Composição do endividamento = PC/Passivo total (41% vs 11%); Imobilização do PL = (Inv+Imob+Intang)/PL (71% vs 210%; >100% means fixed assets financed with third-party funds).
- Solvency: Liquidez corrente = AC/PC (1.6 vs 1.9); Seca = (AC - estoques)/PC (1.3 vs 1.2; ranking flips when inventories removed); Geral = (AC+RLP)/(PC+PNC) (1.0 vs 0.6).
- Profitability: Margem bruta (57% vs 65%), operacional (15% vs 8%), líquida (9.3% vs -2.6%), ROA = LL/Ativos totais (9% vs -1.9%), ROE = LL/PL médio (29% vs -10%). Higher gross margin does not mean higher net margin.
- Exercise structures: Home&Lux, BourtonX, BookCommerce cases (content in figures only).
- App implications: implement vertical/horizontal analysis as automated flags; use average balances (PL médio) for ROE/ROA; compute PMR/PME/PMP with 360 days to match Brazil convention; separate financial-arm receivables for retailers; adjust ratios for non-recurring "outras receitas".

## Book synthesis
1. Parse and normalize CVM/IFRS statements consistently (DFP/ITR): use net revenue as base for margins; separate operating from financial result; strip non-recurring "outras receitas/despesas operacionais", provisions reversals and asset-sale gains before computing EBIT/margins (large effect shown: net margin 16% -> 26% from one-off items).
2. Earnings quality: cross-check NI with CFO via indirect-method bridge (NI + D&A - dNWC); flag CFO << NI, tax provisions unpaid, and working-capital swings that flatter CFO (growing payables).
3. Use average balances (saldo médio) for ROE, ROA, turnover and days; formulas: ROE=LL/PL médio, ROA=LL/Ativo total, PME/PMR/PMP on 360 days, Liquidez corrente/seca/geral.
4. Compare within sector and with benchmarks only: examples show the same retail sector has PME 98 vs 253 days and gross margin 57% vs 65%; sector percentiles are mandatory (banks, utilities, retail all different).
5. Leverage metrics to compute: Capital de terceiros/PL, Passivo exigível/Ativo, composição do endividamento (short-term debt share), imobilização do PL (>100% = fixed assets funded by third parties), net debt/EBITDA; high ST debt share with low cash = refinancing risk (important in high-Selic Brazil).
6. Retail and financial-arm quirks: captive credit receivables inflate PMR; banks/insurers excluded from CCC/EV multiples; use ROE vs Ke and P/B for banks.
7. Balance-sheet quality: goodwill/ágio and intangibles inflate book; compute tangible book; historical cost accounting understates old real assets (land) while IFRS fair-value reserves (ajustes de avaliação patrimonial, outros resultados abrangentes) can swing PL (-595% in example) - adjust P/B interpretation.
8. BR-specific hidden items: "Outros" balance lines growing sharply, provisões para contingências/depósitos judiciais (tax/labor litigation), IR/CSLL 34% statutory, taxes on sales (ICMS/PIS/COFINS) in deductions, participações estatutárias; scandal precedents (Panamericano - sold credit portfolios kept on books) justify checks on bank asset quality and auditor.
9. Going-concern / liquidity cross-check: Liquidez seca can reverse the ranking vs corrente (inventory-heavy firms); use alongside cash conversion cycle.
10. Indicator hygiene: always consistent period definitions (trailing 12M for ITR), handle consolidated vs parent-only, minorities (participação de não controladores) in PL and EV.
